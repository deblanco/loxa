iOS prototype · hair try-on · v1

Loxa — try on any hair before the scissors

Warm-paper light theme, black controls, editorial serif headings. The phone on the left is live: swipe models, pick a style and a colour, hit Try On, watch it generate, share, and walk the profile / camera / paywall branches.

Assumptions
$9.99/week = 20 photos, expiring weekly (shown as "resets Monday, no roll-over"). Out of credits → $0.99 single photo. Daily notification is opt-in on the profile.
System
Paper #faf8f5 / ink #0d0c0b. One weight of black for every control: pills, sheets, chips. Instrument Serif for statements, Instrument Sans for UI, mono for meta.
Imagery
Every photo/video is a labelled placeholder — drop in real footage of people restyling their hair and the whole app snaps into place.
